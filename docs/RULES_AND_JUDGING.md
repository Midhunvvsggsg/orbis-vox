# Rules and judging (NASA Space Apps 2026)

## Deadline
Submission closes Sunday, November 15, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. local time of the team's Local Event (Universal Event: Team Owner's local time). Only the last saved submission is judged. Resubmitting is allowed until the cutoff.

## Eligibility for Global Judging
- Responds to an official challenge (Earth Jukebox).
- All members registered for the same Local Event (not waitlisted) and listed on the Members tab. Local Event teams: 1 to 6 people. Universal Event teams: 2 to 6.
- All required form fields complete, all in English.
- Demo: video of 30 seconds or less, OR slides up to 7 including title and intro. Exceeding either makes the project ineligible. A non-English video needs English subtitles.
- Both checkboxes ticked (terms; original work with all external resources listed in References).
- No likeness of anyone under 18.
- Must use at least one NASA data resource to be eligible for a Global Award.
- Links must be public with no login, permission or registration. Test in a private window.

## Judging criteria (each scored 1 to 5)
| Criterion | What earns a 5 | How ORBIS VOX answers it |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | Significant problem, broad audience | Opens EIC science to blind and low-vision users and gives everyone a new way to feel Earth change |
| Creativity | Novel, inventive | Jukebox/record metaphor, per-dataset "sound personality", live frame-by-frame sonification, exploration probe |
| Validity | Scientifically sound, feasible, usable | Documented, consistent mapping cards; colorbar inversion where available; honest about limits; working public site |
| Relevance | NASA data is core, not decoration | Every sound is computed from real EIC frames; sources named per track |
| Presentation | Clear, structured, compelling story | One-tap listening, 60-second tour mode, tight demo, readable mapping cards |

## Global awards worth targeting
Best Use of Data, Best Storytelling, Art & Technology, Galactic Impact, Global Connection, Most Inspirational. Do not claim an award in the UI.

## SI (AI) rules
- AI tools are allowed, but must not be used to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge.
- Generated images and video need a visible watermark. Audio, code and data must be acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata.
- No NASA branding in generated content.
- Our approach: code is AI-assisted and acknowledged (file headers, README, About page). Audio is algorithmic synthesis from NASA frames, not an AI audio model. No AI-generated images.
