About ORBIS VOX

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: Earth Jukebox

ORBIS VOX transforms authentic NASA Earth Information Center (EIC) and Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) observations into accessible, live musical soundscapes synthesized directly in your browser.

Data Integrity & Transparency

All frames are authentic NASA SVS products. Sonification operates on rendered RGB pixel values, color hues, and spatial features to create distinct musical personalities.

Accessibility First

Designed for full keyboard operability, screen readers, WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, and sight-independent exploration through Listen-Only mode and probe sonification.
